# 02 — Reuse axis in the brief

Status: open
Type: grilling
Blocked by:

## Question

The Claude doc's first criterion for when an artifact is worth creating: "C'est quelque chose que vous voudrez probablement modifier, itérer ou réutiliser en dehors de la conversation" — worth modifying, iterating, or reusing outside the conversation. Current artifacts are one-shot lessons; the brief (gate 1) never asks what the artifact will be *used for later*.

Should the brief gain a **reuse question** ("what will someone do with this after the lesson — rerun it, fork it, quote a value from it?") that shapes the build — e.g. copyable readouts, exportable states, parameter presets? Or is one-shot by design, and the criterion is satisfied by the artifact's own re-readability?

Decide: add the reuse axis to gate 1 / leave the brief as-is / scope reuse to specific artifact classes only.
